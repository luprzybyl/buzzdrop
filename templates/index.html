How it works

Three steps, and nothing left behind.

1

Encrypt before upload

Pick a file or write a note, set a password, and the browser seals it with AES-GCM locally.

2

Share the one-time link

Send the link, and send the password somewhere else. Two channels, one drop.

3

Watch it self-destruct

After the first successful open, the encrypted payload is deleted automatically.
